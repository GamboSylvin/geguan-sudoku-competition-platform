  - **Backend `competition` module:** `POST /api/competitions` (name, description, categories → the `Competition` row `status = CREATED`, one `CompetitionCategory` per category, the 1:1 `ScoringConfiguration` with defaults coefficient `0.6` / ranking cycle `180`, and the fixed structure — two `Stage` rows `INDIVIDUAL`→`TEAM`, two `Round`s each, each with its `RoundSettings` at the documented defaults: `durationSeconds` 1200/1800/1800/1800, `preparationSeconds` 60, `earlyBonusRate` 3, no cap, team rotation defaults); `PATCH /api/competitions/:id` (name/description any time; categories pre-publish only; numeric round settings any time within this unit's scope); `POST /api/competitions/:id/publish`.
